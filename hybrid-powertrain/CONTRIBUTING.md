# Contributing

High-value contributions:
- transmission teardown photos with ZF tag visible;
- accurate 8HP70X / 8HP75X / 8P75HZ / 8P75XPH dimensions;
- ATC35L and ATC13 spline measurements;
- F04 MSD85_HY CAN captures;
- GA8P75HZ/8P75XPH EGS/EME CAN captures;
- DBC definitions with evidence;
- K0 hydraulic diagrams or verified solenoid mapping;
- auxiliary oil-pump control information;
- F13 battery-shell scan data;
- thermal or structural modelling.

Please tag claims as:
- `CONFIRMED`
- `STRONG-INDICATION`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `REJECTED`

Do not road-test unvalidated K0 control, brake/regen blending, transmission torque intervention, HV contactor logic, isolation monitoring or thermal protection. Bench and HIL evidence is preferred first.
